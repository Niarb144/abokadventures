Mt.Kenya National Park

About Mt. Kenya National Park

Mount Kenya National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is home to Africa’s second-highest mountain standing at 5,199 meters. The park showcases breathtaking landscapes ranging from glaciers and alpine meadows to bamboo forests and rich wildlife habitats. It is a sanctuary of biodiversity and cultural heritage, revered by local communities as a sacred site.

Facts about Mt. Kenya National Park

  • Mount Kenya is an extinct volcano formed about 3 million years ago.
  • It has three main peaks: Batian, Nelion, and Point Lenana – with Point Lenana being the most popular for trekkers.
  • The park protects endemic species like the Mount Kenya rock hyrax and rare plant life such as giant lobelias and senecios.
  • Recognized by UNESCO for both its natural beauty and cultural significance.

Activities

Wildlife Safaris & Bird Watching

The lower slopes of the mountain are rich in wildlife. Visitors can spot elephants, buffalo, leopards, hyenas, and bushbucks along forest trails. Bird lovers will enjoy sightings of over 160 bird species, including the endangered Abbott’s starling and crowned eagles.

Mountain Climbing and Trekking

Mount Kenya offers routes for every level of climber. Adventurers can challenge themselves with technical climbs up Batian and Nelion peaks, while most visitors opt for the scenic trek to Point Lenana (4,985m), which rewards hikers with breathtaking sunrise views above the clouds. Popular routes include the Sirimon, Naro Moru, and Chogoria trails, each offering unique scenery.

Camping & Nature Walks

Spend a night under the stars at designated campsites while enjoying the crisp mountain air. Guided nature walks through bamboo and montane forests reveal waterfalls, unique alpine plants like giant lobelias, and glacial lakes such as Lake Ellis and Lake Michaelson.

Scenic Photography & Sightseeing

With its dramatic peaks, glaciers, and deep valleys, Mount Kenya is a paradise for photographers. The Urumandi and Nithi waterfalls, alpine lakes, and panoramic viewpoints make for unforgettable captures.

Fishing and Picnicking

Several high-altitude lakes and rivers provide opportunities for trout fishing in pristine, serene settings. Families and groups can also enjoy picnic spots in forested areas with stunning backdrops of the peaks.
