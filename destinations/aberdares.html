Aberdares Ranges

About Aberdare Ranges

The Aberdare Ranges (often called the Aberdare Mountains) are a volcanic mountain range in central Kenya. They stretch about 160 km (100 miles) north to south and form part of the eastern rim of the Great Rift Valley.

Known for its thick bamboo forests, moorlands, waterfalls (like Karuru and Chania Falls), and rich wildlife including elephants, buffaloes, leopards, and the rare bongo antelope.

Facts about Aberdare

  • Highest Peaks: Mount Satima (about 3,999 m) and Mount Kinangop (about 3,906 m).
  • Aberdare National Park: Known for its thick bamboo forests, moorlands, waterfalls (like Karuru and Chania Falls), and rich wildlife including elephants, buffaloes, leopards, and the rare bongo antelope.
  • Unique Lodges: The famous Treetops Lodge (where Queen Elizabeth II became queen in 1952) is located here.
  • Climate & Scenery: Misty, cool, and lush with streams, ravines, and highland moors — very different from the open savannahs of Maasai Mara.

Activities

Game Viewing Safaris

The Aberdare National Park offers rich wildlife encounters, from large herds of elephants and buffalo to elusive leopards, hyenas, and the rare bongo antelope. Visitors can enjoy guided game drives through diverse landscapes, including bamboo forests, moorlands, and valleys.

Bird Watching

Home to over 250 bird species, the Aberdares is a paradise for bird lovers. Colorful sunbirds, hawks, eagles, and the endangered Aberdare cisticola can be spotted among the lush highland vegetation.

Hiking and Trekking

The cool, misty climate makes the Aberdares ideal for hiking. Trails lead adventurers through dense forests, past crystal-clear streams, and up to panoramic viewpoints on peaks such as Mount Satima and Mount Kinangop.

Waterfall Excursions

The ranges are dotted with spectacular waterfalls, including the famous Karuru Falls (the tallest in Kenya), Chania Falls, and Magura Falls. These scenic spots provide excellent photography opportunities and serene picnic sites.

Lodge and Treehouse Experiences

Visitors can enjoy unique overnight stays at iconic lodges such as The Ark and Treetops Lodge, where wildlife viewing continues into the night as animals gather at nearby waterholes and salt licks.

Fishing and Picnicking

Crystal-clear mountain streams and rivers offer tranquil settings for trout fishing and scenic picnics amidst the forested highlands.
