Maasai Mara Game Reserve

About Maasai Mara

Renowned as one of Africa’s greatest safari destinations, the Maasai Mara is a breathtaking wilderness of endless savannahs, rich wildlife, and vibrant Maasai culture. Famous for the annual Great Wildebeest Migration and its exceptional population of big cats, the Mara offers unmatched game viewing experiences all year round. From thrilling game drives to hot air balloon safaris at sunrise, every visit to the Maasai Mara is an unforgettable journey into the heart of Africa’s natural beauty.

Facts about Maasai Mara

  • Location: Southwestern Kenya, bordering Tanzania’s Serengeti National Park.
  • Size: Covers approximately 1,510 square kilometers.
  • Wildlife: Home to the Big Five (lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino) plus cheetahs, giraffes, wildebeests, zebras, hyenas, hippos, crocodiles, and more than 450 bird species.
  • Predators: Known as the “Kingdom of Big Cats” due to its high concentration of lions, cheetahs, and leopards.
  • Cultural Heritage: Named after the Maasai people, who are native to the region, and the Mara River that flows through the reserve.

Activities

Game Drives

Experience thrilling morning and evening game drives across the vast Mara plains, where sightings of lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, and more await. The reserve’s rich wildlife ensures every safari drive is unique and unforgettable.

The Great Migration Viewing

Witness one of nature’s greatest spectacles — the annual Great Wildebeest Migration (July–October), when millions of wildebeests, zebras, and gazelles cross the Mara River in search of fresh grazing, braving crocodile-infested waters and waiting predators.

Hot Air Balloon Safaris

Float silently above the savannah at sunrise in a hot air balloon, capturing panoramic views of the Mara and its wildlife below. The experience concludes with a champagne breakfast in the bush — a true bucket-list adventure.

Nature & Photography Safaris

From golden sunsets over the savannah to dramatic wildlife action, the Maasai Mara offers endless opportunities for stunning photography and immersive nature experiences.

Cultural Visits

Engage with the Maasai people, known for their colorful attire, warrior traditions, and warm hospitality. Visitors can explore Maasai villages, learn about their customs, and enjoy traditional dance performances.

Bird Watching

With over 450 bird species, including ostriches, secretary birds, eagles, and vultures, the Mara is a paradise for bird enthusiasts. The variety of habitats makes birdwatching rewarding throughout the year.
