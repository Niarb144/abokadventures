3-Day Flying Safari to Tipilikwani Mara Camp

Safari Overview

This 3-day safari takes you straight into the heart of the Maasai Mara via a scenic flight, saving you travel time and maximizing your wildlife adventure. Guests stay at the charming Tipilikwani Mara Camp, offering classic tented luxury, stunning river views, and authentic safari comfort. Expect thrilling game drives, breathtaking sunsets, and encounters with the Big Five.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Maasai Mara (Flight: 1 hr). Scenic aerial views of Rift Valley, transfer to Tipilikwani Mara Camp. Lunch, afternoon game drive. Dinner & overnight at camp (FB).
  • Day 2: Full-day game drive with picnic lunch. Spot lions, elephants, cheetahs, giraffes, hippos. Optional Maasai village visit. Sundowner by the river/campfire. Overnight at Tipilikwani Mara Camp (FB).
  • Day 3: Sunrise game drive. Breakfast at camp. Transfer to airstrip for return flight to Nairobi. End of safari.
