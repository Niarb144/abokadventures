5-Day Honeymoon Flying Safari to Saruni Eagle View

Safari Overview

Celebrate love in the wild with this 5-day luxury honeymoon safari. Fly directly into the Maasai Mara for a seamless and romantic experience at Saruni Eagle View Camp, perched on an escarpment with panoramic views of the Mara. Enjoy champagne welcomes, private game drives, bush dinners under the stars, spa treatments, and unforgettable encounters with wildlife – a magical beginning to your life together.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Maasai Mara (Flight, ~1 hr). Champagne welcome, afternoon game drive & romantic candlelit dinner. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 2: Private sunrise game drive, breakfast with views, optional spa, and private bush dinner. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 3: Optional hot air balloon safari (extra), champagne bush breakfast, Maasai village visit, and sunset cocktails. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 4: Private full-day game drive with picnic lunch, afternoon relaxation, and romantic honeymoon cake surprise. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 5: Sunrise game drive, breakfast at camp, and return flight to Nairobi. End of safari.

Pricing

Rates are per person sharing (honeymoon extras included):
