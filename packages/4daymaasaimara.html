4-Day Masai Mara Safari

Safari Overview

Embark on an unforgettable 4-day adventure to the Masai Mara, Kenya’s premier wildlife reserve. This safari takes you through the scenic Great Rift Valley and into the heart of the Mara, home to the legendary Big Five and the annual Great Wildebeest Migration. Perfect for couples, families, and small groups, this package blends luxury and adventure with exceptional game drives and cultural encounters.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Masai Mara (Drive through the Great Rift Valley, afternoon game drive).
  • Day 2: Full-day game drives in the Masai Mara with picnic lunch.
  • Day 3: Sunrise game drive, optional hot air balloon safari, and cultural visit to a Maasai village.
  • Day 4: Morning game drive, return to Nairobi.

Safari Highlights

  • Witness the Big Five in their natural habitat
  • Chance to see the Great Wildebeest Migration (July–October)
  • Panoramic drives through the Great Rift Valley
  • Optional hot air balloon safari at sunrise
  • Cultural interaction with the Maasai people

Optional Activities
