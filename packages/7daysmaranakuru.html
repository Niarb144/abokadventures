7-Day Masai Mara – Lake Nakuru – Takawiri Island Safari

Safari Overview

This 7-day adventure takes you from the world-famous Masai Mara to the flamingo-lined Lake Nakuru and finally to the serene Takawiri Island on Lake Victoria. Experience thrilling game drives, bird watching, and beach relaxation all in one safari package. Perfect for travelers who want both adventure and tranquility in East Africa.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Masai Mara (Rift Valley drive, afternoon game drive).
  • Day 2: Full-day game drives in Masai Mara, optional Maasai village visit.
  • Day 3: Sunrise game drive, optional hot air balloon safari.
  • Day 4: Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru (afternoon game drive, flamingos & rhinos).
  • Day 5: Lake Nakuru → Takawiri Island (drive via Kisumu/Homa Bay, boat transfer).
  • Day 6: Takawiri Island leisure (beach, fishing, bird watching).
  • Day 7: Morning relaxation, return to Nairobi.

Best Time to Visit

  • Masai Mara: July – October (Great Migration) & year-round safaris.
  • Lake Nakuru: Jan–Mar & Jun–Oct (dry seasons).
  • Takawiri Island: Dec–Mar & Jun–Oct (clear skies, calm waters).

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included:

  • Transport in a 4×4 Land Cruiser with driver-guide
  • 6 nights full-board accommodation
  • Park entrance fees (Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru)
  • Unlimited game drives
  • Boat transfers to/from Takawiri
  • Bottled water during game drives
  • Airport pick-up/drop-off

Excluded:

  • International flights & visas
  • Travel insurance & medical cover
  • Optional activities (hot air balloon, Maasai village, fishing)
  • Personal expenses (laundry, drinks, tips, souvenirs)

Safari Highlights

  • See the Big Five & witness the Great Migration
  • Flamingos & rhinos at Lake Nakuru
  • Relax on the white sandy beaches of Takawiri Island
  • Experience Maasai and Luo cultural traditions
  • Combination of safari, lake, and island experiences

Optional Activities

  • Hot air balloon safari over Masai Mara
  • Cultural visit to a Maasai village
  • Fishing & boat rides at Takawiri Island
  • Nature walks & bird watching

Packing List

  • Light clothing & warm jacket for evenings
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses & hat
  • Binoculars & camera
  • Insect repellent
  • Travel documents (passport, visa, insurance)

Health & Safety

  • Yellow fever vaccination may be required
  • Anti-malarial medication recommended
  • Drink bottled or purified water
  • Follow guide instructions during game drives

Cuisine & Dining

During this safari, enjoy a mix of international and local cuisines, with fresh fish at Takawiri Island, bush meals in Masai Mara, and traditional Kenyan dishes. Vegetarian and dietary options are available on request.
