12-Day Kenya & Tanzania Group Joining Safari

Safari Overview

Embark on an extraordinary 12-day journey through Kenya and Tanzania, discovering East Africa’s most iconic national parks and reserves. This group joining safari combines breathtaking landscapes, thrilling game drives, cultural interactions, and unique wildlife encounters. From the Maasai Mara and Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater and Amboseli, this itinerary offers the ultimate safari adventure.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi – Transfer to hotel and safari briefing.
  • Day 2: Nairobi → Masai Mara – Scenic drive, afternoon game drive.
  • Day 3: Full-day game drives in Masai Mara with picnic lunch.
  • Day 4: Masai Mara → Serengeti (via Isebania border) – Evening game drive.
  • Day 5: Full-day game drives in Serengeti.
  • Day 6: Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater – Game drive en route.
  • Day 7: Full-day Ngorongoro Crater exploration.
  • Day 8: Ngorongoro → Lake Manyara – Afternoon game drive.
  • Day 9: Lake Manyara → Amboseli – Drive via Namanga border, evening rest.
  • Day 10: Full-day game drives in Amboseli beneath Mt. Kilimanjaro.
  • Day 11: Amboseli → Nairobi – Stopover at attractions, leisure time.
  • Day 12: Departure – Airport transfer for your flight home.

Best Time to Travel

The best time for this safari is between June and October during the Great Wildebeest Migration and December to March for excellent wildlife viewing across both Kenya and Tanzania. However, the parks are rewarding year-round with diverse landscapes and abundant wildlife.

Travel Tips

Pack lightweight clothing, sturdy walking shoes, sunscreen, insect repellent, and a camera with extra memory cards. Binoculars are highly recommended for birdwatching and distant wildlife sightings.

Health & Safety

Travelers are advised to carry malaria prophylaxis, stay hydrated, and follow the guide’s instructions at all times for a safe safari experience.

Cultural Experiences

Engage in meaningful interactions with local Maasai and other East African communities. Respect traditions and always ask before taking photos.

Cuisine & Dining

Enjoy a mix of international cuisine and traditional African dishes. Expect fresh fruits, grilled meats, and local specialties in your lodges and camps.

Conservation & Sustainability

This safari supports eco-friendly camps and lodges that contribute to wildlife conservation and community empowerment.

Photography Opportunities

Capture unforgettable moments: wildebeest migration crossings, Ngorongoro’s crater floor, Amboseli’s elephants, and Serengeti’s endless plains at sunrise.
