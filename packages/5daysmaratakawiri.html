7-Day Masai Mara – Takawiri Island Safari

Safari Overview

This safari combines the thrill of the Masai Mara with the tranquility of Takawiri Island on Lake Victoria. Begin your journey with unforgettable game drives in the Mara, then unwind on pristine sandy beaches with options for fishing, boat rides, and bird watching. Operated by Abok Adventures & Safaris, this package blends adventure, culture, and relaxation for the perfect East African experience.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Masai Mara (Pick up, drive/flight to Mara, afternoon game drive. Overnight: Mid-range Mara Lodge/Camp).
  • Day 2: Masai Mara – Full day game drives. Optional hot air balloon safari (USD $450 pp). Overnight: Mara Lodge/Camp.
  • Day 3: Masai Mara → Takawiri Island (Road/flight + boat transfer. Evening leisure by the beach. Overnight: Takawiri Island Resort).
  • Day 4: Takawiri Island – Leisure and activities (boat rides, fishing, bird watching, beach relaxation). Overnight: Takawiri Island Resort.
  • Day 5: Takawiri → Nairobi (Boat + road/flight back. Drop-off at hotel/airport).

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included:

  • 4 nights mid-range accommodation (full board)
  • All park entry fees
  • Transport in 4×4 safari vehicle with guide
  • Boat transfers to Takawiri Island
  • Unlimited game drives in Masai Mara
  • Bottled mineral water during safari

Excluded:

  • International flights
  • Optional balloon safari (USD $450 pp)

  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Travel insurance

Safari Highlights

  • Game drives in the Masai Mara with chances to spot the Big Five
  • Relaxation on Takawiri Island’s white sandy beaches
  • Fishing, bird watching, and boat rides on Lake Victoria
  • Combination of safari adventure and lakeside tranquility
