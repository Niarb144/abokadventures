10-Day Kenya & Tanzania Safari

Safari Overview

This cross-border journey links Kenya’s Lake Nakuru and Maasai Mara with Tanzania’s Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, and Tarangire—five of East Africa’s most iconic ecosystems. Expect spectacular game viewing, dramatic landscapes, and a balanced rhythm of travel days and immersive game drives.

Perfect for couples, families, and small groups seeking Big Five sightings, seasonal Great Migration encounters, and rich cultural interaction—all while staying in comfortable lodges/tented camps and traveling in custom 4×4 vehicles with pop-up roofs.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi — Meet & greet, transfer to hotel, safari briefing. Overnight in Nairobi (B&B).
  • Day 2: Nairobi → Lake Nakuru — Drive to Nakuru; afternoon game drive (flamingos, rhinos). Overnight near Lake Nakuru.
  • Day 3: Lake Nakuru → Maasai Mara — Rift Valley views, evening game drive. Overnight in Mara lodge/camp.
  • Day 4: Maasai Mara — Full day game drives; optional Maasai village visit. Overnight in Mara.
  • Day 5: Mara → Serengeti (via Isebania) — Border crossing; first Serengeti game drive. Overnight in Serengeti.
  • Day 6: Serengeti — Full day safari; seasonal Great Migration & predators. Overnight in Serengeti.
  • Day 7: Serengeti → Ngorongoro Area — Morning game drive; transfer to crater rim lodge.
  • Day 8: Ngorongoro Crater — Descend 600 m for game drive (rhino, lions, elephants, hippos). Overnight at Ngorongoro.
  • Day 9: Ngorongoro → Tarangire — Afternoon game drive among elephants & baobabs. Overnight in Tarangire.
  • Day 10: Tarangire → Arusha — Morning game drive; transfer to Arusha/Kilimanjaro Airport for departure.

Best Time to Travel

  • June – October: Prime dry season, excellent wildlife viewing across both countries.
  • July – October: Great Migration viewing in Maasai Mara/Serengeti corridors.
  • December – March: Serengeti calving season with dramatic predator action.

Travel Tips & Health

What to Pack

  • Neutral clothing, comfy shoes, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen.
  • Camera with spare batteries/memory cards; light jacket for cool mornings/evenings.
  • Passport/visa/travel insurance documents.

Health & Safety

  • Comprehensive travel insurance required (medical, evacuation, trip cover).
  • Recommended vaccines: yellow fever, typhoid, hepatitis; malaria prophylaxis advised.
  • Bottled drinking water provided throughout the safari.

Conservation & Culture

Your visit supports conservation via park fees; many partner lodges practice eco-measures like solar power and responsible waste management, while cultural visits help sustain local livelihoods.
