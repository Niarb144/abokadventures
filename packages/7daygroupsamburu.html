7-Day Samburu to Mara Safari

Safari Overview

This 7-day group safari takes you on a thrilling journey across Kenya’s most iconic parks: Samburu National Reserve, Ol Pejeta Conservancy, Lake Naivasha, and the world-famous Masai Mara. Experience breathtaking landscapes, diverse cultures, and incredible wildlife encounters including the Big Five and the unique Samburu “Special Five.”

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Samburu (drive through Mount Kenya region, afternoon game drive).
  • Day 2: Full-day game drives in Samburu to spot elephants, lions, and rare species like Grevy’s zebra.
  • Day 3: Samburu → Ol Pejeta Conservancy (visit the only chimpanzee sanctuary in Kenya, and rhino sanctuary).
  • Day 4: Ol Pejeta → Lake Naivasha (boat ride on the lake, Crescent Island walking safari).
  • Day 5: Lake Naivasha → Masai Mara (evening game drive upon arrival).
  • Day 6: Full-day game drive in Masai Mara with picnic lunch in the savannah.
  • Day 7: Morning game drive, then return to Nairobi in the afternoon.

Best Time to Travel

The safari is available year-round, but the best experiences are:

  • July – October: Great Wildebeest Migration in Masai Mara.
  • December – March: Excellent game viewing and pleasant weather.
  • April – June: Low season with fewer crowds, lush scenery, and discounted rates.
