4-Day Flying Safari to Saruni Eagle View – Mara Naboisho

Safari Overview

This 4-day fly-in safari takes you to the pristine Mara Naboisho Conservancy, bordering the Maasai Mara. Stay at the award-winning Saruni Eagle View Camp, a luxury eco-lodge with panoramic views of the savannah. Experience exclusive conservancy game drives, guided walking safaris, night drives, and cultural encounters with the Maasai community. A perfect blend of luxury, conservation, and culture.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Mara Naboisho (Flight ~1 hr). En-route game drive to Saruni Eagle View. Afternoon game drive in the conservancy. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 2: Morning game drive, mid-morning walking safari with Maasai guides. Relax at camp. Evening night game drive. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 3: Full-day game drive in Maasai Mara National Reserve with picnic lunch. Sundowners at camp. Overnight: Saruni Eagle View (FB).
  • Day 4: Sunrise game drive and bush breakfast. Flight back to Nairobi. Drop-off at Wilson Airport or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.
