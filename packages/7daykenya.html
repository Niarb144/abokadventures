7-Day Kenya Safari: Masai Mara • Lake Nakuru • Amboseli

Safari Overview

Experience Kenya’s most iconic wildlife destinations in this 7-day safari, combining the world-renowned Masai Mara, the birdwatcher’s paradise of Lake Nakuru, and the elephant haven of Amboseli with stunning views of Mt. Kilimanjaro. Operated by Abok Adventures & Safaris, this package blends game drives, breathtaking scenery, and cultural encounters in one seamless journey. Starting and ending in Nairobi, it’s perfect for travelers seeking the ultimate Kenyan safari circuit.

Book This Safari

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Nairobi → Masai Mara (Scenic drive via Great Rift Valley, afternoon game drive. Overnight: Mid-range lodge/camp in Masai Mara).
  • Day 2: Masai Mara – Full day game drives. Optional hot air balloon safari ($450 pp). Overnight: Masai Mara Lodge/Camp.
  • Day 3: Masai Mara – Explore more of the reserve, including Mara River (migration point in season). Overnight: Masai Mara Lodge/Camp.
  • Day 4: Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru (Drive in the morning, afternoon game drive with flamingos, rhinos, giraffes. Overnight: Lake Nakuru Lodge).
  • Day 5: Nakuru → Amboseli (Drive south with Kilimanjaro views. Afternoon game drive. Overnight: Amboseli Lodge/Camp).
  • Day 6: Amboseli – Full day safari with large elephant herds and Kilimanjaro backdrop. Overnight: Amboseli Lodge/Camp.
  • Day 7: Amboseli → Nairobi (Morning game drive, breakfast, return to Nairobi. Drop-off at hotel/airport).
