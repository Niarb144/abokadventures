Olare Mara Kempinski

About Olare Mara Kempinski

Olare Mara Kempinski offers an exclusive luxury tented camp experience in the heart of the Masai Mara ecosystem. Situated within the Olare Motorogi Conservancy, the camp is renowned for its unmatched privacy, rich wildlife viewing, and world-class hospitality. Each tent is carefully designed to blend luxury with an authentic bush experience, offering guests an intimate connection with nature.

With only 12 tents, Olare Mara Kempinski ensures personalized service, tranquil seclusion, and access to some of the best predator sightings in the region.

Facts about Olare Mara Kempinski

  • Located in Olare Motorogi Conservancy, adjacent to Masai Mara National Reserve.
  • Only 12 luxury tents for a private and intimate safari experience.
  • Known for exceptional predator sightings, including lions, leopards, and cheetahs.
  • Elegant tents feature four-poster beds, soaking tubs, and private decks.
  • Infinity swimming pool overlooking the savannah.
  • Strong focus on conservation and eco-friendly practices.

Activities

Game Drives

Enjoy guided drives through Olare Motorogi Conservancy, famous for its high density of big cats and other wildlife.

Sundowners

Unwind with refreshing cocktails and canapés while watching the African sunset over the savannah.

Guided Bush Walks

Take a closer look at the ecosystem with a guided walking safari, learning about plants, birds, and animal tracks.
