JW Marriott

Who we are

JW Marriott Masai Mara Lodge is an exclusive safari retreat set along the Talek River within the world-renowned Masai Mara National Reserve. Surrounded by abundant wildlife and sweeping savannahs, the lodge offers unmatched luxury in the heart of nature. Guests can unwind in lavish tented suites while enjoying panoramic views of roaming elephants, lions, and wildebeest.

Location: Masai Mara National Reserve, Kenya – Riverside setting overlooking the Talek River.

What We Offer

Combining modern elegance with authentic safari experiences, JW Marriott Masai Mara Lodge provides premium amenities and personalized services.

  • Luxury ensuite tented suites with private decks
  • Rain showers & freestanding bathtubs
  • Infinity pool overlooking the savannah
  • Fine dining restaurant & bush dinners
  • Spa and wellness therapies
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi
  • Guided daily game drives

Activities

Game Drives

Experience world-class safari drives led by expert guides, offering close encounters with the Big Five, wildebeest migrations, and stunning savannah landscapes.

Bush Dining

Enjoy private dining experiences under the African sky, from champagne breakfasts in the wild to romantic lantern-lit dinners in the bush.

Cultural Visits

Engage with the Maasai community, learn about their traditions, dances, and way of life while supporting sustainable tourism.

Photography Safaris

Capture breathtaking wildlife moments with guided photography safaris designed for both amateurs and professionals.

Wellness & Spa

Relax at the lodge’s luxury spa with treatments inspired by local traditions, designed to rejuvenate body and mind after a day on safari.

Hot Air Balloon Safaris

Drift silently over the Mara at sunrise, witnessing sweeping views of the plains and wildlife below, followed by a champagne breakfast.
