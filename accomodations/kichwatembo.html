&Beyond Kichwa Tembo Camp

About &Beyond Kichwa Tembo Camp

Nestled on the edge of the Oloololo escarpment, overlooking the endless plains of the Maasai Mara, &Beyond Kichwa Tembo Tented Camp is one of the most iconic safari destinations in East Africa. Known for its sweeping savannah views and proximity to the Great Migration routes, it offers a truly immersive wilderness experience.

The camp features a blend of contemporary design and traditional safari style, with luxury tents, a refreshing pool, and a serene setting perfect for relaxation after adventurous game drives.

Facts about &Beyond Kichwa Tembo Camp

  • Located on the Oloololo Escarpment, Maasai Mara.
  • 40 luxury safari tents with modern amenities.
  • Infinity pool with sweeping views of the Mara.
  • Private verandas with panoramic landscapes.
  • Known for exceptional game viewing, especially during the Great Migration.
  • Eco-friendly operations with community involvement.
