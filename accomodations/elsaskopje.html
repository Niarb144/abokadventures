Elsa's Kopje Camp

About Elsa's Kopje Camp

Nestled in the rugged hills of Meru National Park, Elsa's Kopje Camp is a boutique luxury lodge built around the famous hill where George and Joy Adamson raised the lioness Elsa, star of *Born Free*. The lodge offers sweeping views, unmatched exclusivity, and a strong connection to wildlife conservation.

Each cottage is individually designed and blends seamlessly with the natural rock outcrops, giving guests privacy and breathtaking panoramas of Meru’s diverse landscapes.

Facts about Elsa's Kopje
